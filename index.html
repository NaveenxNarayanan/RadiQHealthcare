How it works

Live in days, on the PACS you already use.

Connect

We set up a secure DICOM link to your existing PACS or modality. You don't need new hardware or need to change your workflow.

Route

Studies arrive with priority and clinical history. Each is routed to the right subspecialist, and STAT cases jump the queue.

Report

Signed reports return to your PACS or RIS. Critical findings are also phoned through to your duty doctor.

Quality

Fast turnaround is only useful if the read is right.

  • Subspecialty matchingNeuro cases go to neuroradiologists, joints to MSK radiologists.
  • Critical findings called, not just writtenStroke, bleeds, PE and other critical findings are phoned to your team within 15 minutes.
  • Peer review and auditDiscrepant and critical cases get a second read. Discrepancy rates are shared in your monthly report.
  • A radiologist you can callYour clinicians can reach the reporting radiologist directly to discuss a case.

FAQ

Questions facilities ask first

Do we need new hardware or software?

No. We connect to your existing PACS or modality over a secure, encrypted DICOM link. Reports come back into the same system your team already uses.

Can you cover only nights and weekends?

Yes. Many facilities use RadiQ for after-hours and holiday cover while their in-house radiologists report during the day. You can also send overflow when volumes spike.

Who signs the reports?

Every report is signed by an MD or DNB radiologist registered with the National Medical Commission. Complex cases go to a subspecialist.

How are critical findings handled?

The reporting radiologist phones your nominated duty doctor as soon as a critical finding is identified. The call is logged against the case, as well as written in the report.

How is pricing structured?

Per study, by modality and turnaround priority, with volume tiers. Request sample reports and we'll send a quote for your mix.

How is patient data protected?

Studies are encrypted in transit and at rest, access is role-based and logged, and we handle personal data in line with the DPDP Act, 2023. We sign a data processing agreement with every client.
